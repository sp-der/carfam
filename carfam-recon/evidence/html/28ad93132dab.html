Description

The 2020 Ram 1500 Laramie 4x2 Crew Cab 5'7" Box stands out as a premier choice for truck buyers seeking a seamless blend of rugged capability and upscale sophistication. Dressed in a striking Bright White Clearcoat exterior, this half-ton pickup commands attention on the road, especially when paired with the bold styling cues of the Sport Appearance Package. The spacious Crew Cab configuration ensures there is plenty of room for family, friends, or crew members, while the versatile 5'7" utility box offers the ideal amount of cargo space for daily hauling needs.

Under the hood lies the heart of this truck's impressive capability: the powerful 5.7L V8 HEMI MDS VVT eTorque engine. This advanced mild-hybrid powertrain delivers the classic V8 muscle and exhaust note drivers love, enhanced with eTorque technology for optimized efficiency, smooth start-stop operation, and extra low-end launch torque. Combined with a capable 4x2 drivetrain, this powertrain setup offers excellent towing and payload capacity, making light work of trailers, boats, or heavy payloads while maintaining a smooth and composed ride on both highway stretches and city streets.

Inside, the cabin transforms into a luxurious sanctuary featuring premium Black leather-trimmed bucket seats that provide exceptional support for long journeys. The technological centerpiece of the interior is the high-definition Radio: Uconnect 12 with navigation, boasting a massive 12-inch touchscreen that makes routing, media controls, and vehicle settings effortlessly accessible. Further elevating your driving experience is the Laramie Level 1 Equipment Group, adding an extra layer of premium convenience and comfort features to this already well-appointed truck. Do not miss the opportunity to park this highly capable and incredibly stylish 2020 Ram 1500 Laramie in your driveway today.
