Description

Experience the perfect blend of luxury and engaging performance with this stunning 2011 Lexus IS 250 4dr Sport Sdn Man RWD. Finished in a sleek Tungsten Pearl exterior, this sport sedan commands attention on the road with its athletic stance and refined styling. Designed for driving enthusiasts who appreciate the rare connection of a manual transmission paired with rear-wheel drive, this vehicle offers a sophisticated aesthetic coupled with a spirited, hands-on driving dynamic.

Under the hood, the 2011 Lexus IS 250 is powered by a responsive 2.5L 6-cylinder engine that delivers smooth and reliable power. The rear-wheel-drive configuration and precise manual shifter put you in complete control of your drive, transforming daily commutes into engaging journeys. Enhancing its sporty performance and road presence are the 18" liquid graphite alloy wheels wrapped in summer tires, while integrated fog lights ensure excellent visibility and a sharp look in diverse driving conditions.

Inside the cabin, you are welcomed by a refined Black interior featuring premium leather seats and power-adjustable front seats for tailored comfort. This model comes well-equipped with modern conveniences, including keyless entry, automatic climate control, telematics, Bluetooth connectivity, and a premium sound system. Enhanced by the Premium Pkg Value Edition, the Preferred Accessory Package, and a comprehensive safety pkg, this 2011 Lexus IS 250 stands as an exceptional, highly desirable package for those seeking an upscale, driver-focused sport sedan.
