Description

The 2019 Chevrolet Colorado 4WD Ext Cab 128.3" Z71 is a versatile and robust midsize pickup that stands out with its striking Summit White exterior. Complementing its athletic aesthetics, the interior boasts a Jet Black color scheme that adds a touch of sophistication and comfort. Under the hood, it features a powerful 3.6L V6 engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, ensuring a smooth and responsive driving experience whether you're on or off the road.

Equipped with a comprehensive set of advanced features, the Colorado Z71 enhances both convenience and utility. The wireless charging capability and Bluetooth integration provide seamless connectivity, while the heated seats ensure a comfortable ride in various weather conditions. For added versatility, the vehicle is outfitted with a heavy-duty trailering package and integrated trailer brake controller, making it ideal for towing and hauling needs.

Designed for an active lifestyle, this pickup comes with a durable spray-on bedliner emblazoned with the Chevrolet logo, offering protection against the toughest of loads. The advanced suspension and towing package, along with four-wheel drive capability, enable superior performance on rugged terrains. Features like remote start and multi-zone auto climate control add to the convenience and luxury of owning this vehicle. Safety doesn't take a back seat with a backup camera included, providing enhanced visibility in tight spaces. Overall, this Colorado model perfectly blends functionality, comfort, and style, making it a solid choice for those who seek adventure and practicality.
