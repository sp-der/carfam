Description

The 2020 Cadillac XT5 AWD 4dr Premium Luxury in Crystal White Tricoat offers a sophisticated exterior paired with a luxurious Jet Black interior, making a striking presence on the road. Powered by a robust 3.6L V6 engine with DI VVT and automatic stop/start technology, this SUV delivers exceptional performance along with enhanced fuel efficiency.

Equipped with all-wheel drive capabilities, this model ensures superior handling and control in a variety of road conditions. The spacious interior features a panoramic sunroof, tri-zone automatic climate control, and ventilated seats, providing both comfort and style for every journey.

Safety and technology go hand in hand with the Cadillac User Experience, offering integrated navigation, Bluetooth connectivity, and wireless charging. Enhanced features like a blind spot monitor, forward collision warning, and rain-sensing wipers contribute to a safer driving experience. With heated seats and a smart towing package, the XT5 accommodates both comfort and versatility, making it a standout choice for discerning drivers.
