Description

Step into premium comfort and capability with this stunning 2019 Chevrolet Suburban 4WD 4dr 1500 LT. Finished in a sophisticated Satin Steel Metallic exterior and paired with a refined Jet Black/Dark Ash interior, this full-size SUV commands a strong presence on the road while offering the ultimate space for passengers and cargo. Perfect for growing families and adventure seekers alike, this Suburban LT combines legendary styling with modern utility, making it the ideal choice for those who refuse to compromise on passenger comfort or cargo capacity.

Beneath the hood, this 2019 Chevrolet Suburban is powered by a robust 5.3L 8-cylinder engine paired with an advanced Four Wheel Drive drivetrain. This powerful combination delivers reliable performance and maximum traction, giving you peace of mind whether navigating slick winter highways or heading off the beaten path. For those with heavy-duty needs, this vehicle comes equipped with the Max Trailering Package, significantly enhancing its towing capability so you can confidently haul trailers, boats, or campers on your next weekend getaway.

Inside, this SUV is packed with premium features designed to elevate every journey, starting with the highly desirable Sun, Entertainment, and Destinations Package. Passengers in the back will stay thoroughly engaged during long road trips thanks to the rear seat Blu-ray/DVD entertainment system, while the power tilt-sliding sunroof opens up the cabin to beautiful sky views. Riding on striking 20" x 9" polished-aluminum wheels, this 2019 Chevrolet Suburban 1500 LT perfectly balances upscale luxury with rugged utility, making it the ultimate all-purpose vehicle ready for your driveway today.
