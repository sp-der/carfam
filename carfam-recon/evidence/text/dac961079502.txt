Description
Experience the perfect blend of sport luxury and timeless elegance with this stunning 2012 INFINITI G37 Sedan Journey RWD. Dressed in a gorgeous Moonlight White exterior that beautifully contrasts with its premium Graphite interior, this luxury sedan commands attention on any road. Designed for discerning drivers who crave both sophisticated styling and everyday versatility, this G37 Journey offers an upscale cabin experience and a sleek, athletic silhouette that remains highly desirable.
Under the hood, this 2012 INFINITI G37 Sedan is powered by a robust 3.7L 6-cylinder engine, delivering exhilarating acceleration and a refined power delivery. Sending power directly to the rear wheels (RWD), this sedan offers dynamic handling and an engaging driving experience that enthusiasts appreciate. The driving dynamics and exterior styling are further enhanced by the factory-equipped [w01] Performance Tire & Wheel Package, ensuring confident grip, precise steering feedback, and a sporty stance.
Inside, this Journey trim is loaded with premium amenities designed to elevate your daily drive. The cabin features the stylish [x01] Interior Accent Package and elegant [n92] Illuminated Kick Plates that welcome you upon entry. Drivers will benefit from the advanced [u01] Navigation Package for seamless route planning, while the [p01] Premium Package enhances comfort and overall convenience. Combining athletic rear-wheel-drive performance with high-end technology, this 2012 INFINITI G37 Journey is the ideal choice for those seeking a premium sedan that delivers on every front.
