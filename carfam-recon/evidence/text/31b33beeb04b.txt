Description
Step into a sophisticated blend of efficiency and style with this premium 2017 Ford Fusion Energi Platinum FWD. Designed for those who refuse to compromise on elegance or eco-conscious performance, this plug-in hybrid sedan stands out with its gorgeous White Platinum Metallic Tri-Coat exterior finish. Offering an elevated driving experience, this vehicle is the perfect choice for buyers looking to secure a modern, high-quality sedan that makes a statement on the road.
At the heart of this vehicle's capability is an advanced powertrain featuring a 2.0L iVCT Atkinson Cycle I-4 Hybrid engine. This efficient 2.0L 4-cylinder setup seamlessly coordinates with the plug-in hybrid system to deliver smooth power and excellent efficiency. Combined with a refined front-wheel-drive (FWD) system, this powertrain delivers a quiet, composed, and responsive ride that makes navigating daily traffic or cruising on the highway an absolute pleasure.
Inside, the cabin wraps you in comfort with its beautiful Medium Soft Ceramic interior color scheme, creating an inviting and upscale atmosphere. The exterior design is beautifully complemented by eye-catching 18" machine-faced aluminum wheels with painted pockets, and the vehicle also comes equipped with a practical front license plate bracket and the premium Equipment Group 950A. If you are searching for a well-appointed hybrid that checks every box for style, comfort, and efficiency, this 2017 Ford Fusion Energi Platinum FWD is ready for your next journey.
