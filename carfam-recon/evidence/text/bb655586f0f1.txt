Description
Discover the perfect blend of modern style and family-friendly utility with this stunning 2019 Chevrolet Traverse FWD 4dr LT Cloth w/1LT. Finished in a sophisticated Mosaic Black Metallic exterior that commands attention on any road, this midsize SUV opens up to a refined Jet Black cloth interior designed for comfort and durability. Whether you are navigating daily school runs or embarking on a weekend road trip, this versatile vehicle offers the spaciousness and sleek styling that modern drivers demand.
Under the hood, this 2019 Chevrolet Traverse is powered by a robust 3.6L 6-cylinder engine, delivering smooth, confident acceleration and reliable performance for all your journeys. This front-wheel-drive (FWD) SUV is engineered to handle your active lifestyle, featuring the LPO 'Hit the Road' Package for enhanced utility. With LPO molded assist steps for easy cabin entry and LPO black roof rack cross rails to secure your extra gear, this Traverse is fully prepared for both daily errands and highway adventures.
Inside, the cabin prioritizes comfort, cleanliness, and peace of mind with the inclusion of the Convenience and Driver Confidence Package alongside the protective LPO Floor Liner Package. These thoughtfully selected groups ensure that your interior remains pristine while providing driver-assist features to keep you secure on the road. Offering a complete blend of capability, safety-minded technology, and bold styling, this 2019 Chevrolet Traverse LT is the ultimate upgrade for your driveway—schedule your test drive today.
