Description
Experience the pinnacle of modern luxury with this stunning 2022 Cadillac Escalade 4WD 4dr Sport. Draped in an exquisite Crystal White Tricoat exterior, this premium full-size SUV commands attention on any road with its bold, athletic stance and sophisticated dark trim accents characteristic of the Sport styling. Inside, the cabin welcomes you with an exceptionally curated Brandy with Very Dark Atmosphere accent interior, delivering a striking contrast that defines upscale automotive artistry. For discerning buyers seeking a vehicle that seamlessly merges dominant road presence with refined elegance, this Escalade stands as the ultimate statement of luxury and style.
Beneath the sculpted hood lies a formidable 6.2L 8-cylinder engine that delivers effortless acceleration and commanding power whenever you step on the gas. Paired with a robust Four Wheel Drive (4WD) system, this 2022 Cadillac Escalade ensures confident traction and stability across diverse road conditions and seasonal weather. This model is also equipped with the Heavy-Duty Trailering Package, significantly enhancing its utility and making it as capable of hauling your favorite recreational toys as it is navigating downtown streets. Whether handling daily commutes or embarking on a long-distance road trip, its powertrain and towing capabilities offer a driving experience defined by strength and composure.
Comfort and innovation take center stage inside the cabin, highlighted by the ultra-premium Brandy with Very Dark Atmosphere accents full leather seats featuring elegant faceted quilting that cradles passengers in absolute luxury. This 2022 Cadillac Escalade is also equipped with Cadillac's revolutionary Super Cruise hands-free driver assistance technology, transforming long highway drives into relaxing, stress-free journeys. Complete with practical additions like a front license plate bracket, this meticulously designed SUV leaves no detail overlooked. Do not miss the opportunity to elevate your lifestyle—contact us today to experience this exceptional blend of capability, comfort, and advanced technology firsthand.
