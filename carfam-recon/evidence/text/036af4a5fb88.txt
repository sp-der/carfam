Description
If you are searching for a stylish, modern sedan that stands out on the road, this Carfax 1-Owner 2021 Kia K5 GT-Line Auto FWD is an exceptional choice. Finished in a stunning Glacial White Pearl exterior, this vehicle perfectly captures a sporty yet sophisticated aesthetic. Choosing a Carfax 1-Owner vehicle provides added confidence in its history, making this highly desirable sedan an attractive opportunity for buyers seeking modern curb appeal, reliability, and pride of ownership.
Under the hood, the 2021 Kia K5 GT-Line is powered by an efficient and responsive 1.6L 4-cylinder engine paired with a smooth automatic transmission. This front-wheel-drive (FWD) configuration delivers a balanced and composed ride, ensuring confident handling whether you are navigating tight city streets or cruising on the highway. Engineered to offer an engaging driving experience, this powertrain offers the perfect blend of performance and everyday driving efficiency.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a refined black interior featuring comfortable black cloth/leatherette seat trim that enhances the sporty vibe of the GT-Line. This vehicle also comes equipped with practical additions, including carpeted floor mats to protect the footwells and wheel locks for added security and peace of mind. With its striking Glacial White Pearl styling, efficient powertrain, and clean history, this 2021 Kia K5 GT-Line is ready to elevate your daily commute.
