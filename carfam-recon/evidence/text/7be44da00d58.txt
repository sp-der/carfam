Description
Step up to the perfect blend of bold styling and everyday utility with this striking 2021 Ram 1500 Big Horn 4x2 Crew Cab 5'7" Box. Finished in a head-turning Billet Silver Metallic Clearcoat and enhanced by the aggressive, blacked-out aesthetic of the Night Edition, this truck commands attention on the road. Whether you are seeking a commanding daily driver or a versatile companion for weekend adventures, the spacious Crew Cab configuration provides ample passenger comfort alongside a highly functional 5'7" utility box.
Under the hood, this pickup is powered by the legendary 5.7L V8 HEMI MDS VVT engine, delivering the robust horsepower and torque necessary for confident hauling and towing. This advanced 8-cylinder powertrain utilizes Fuel Saver Technology (MDS) to optimize efficiency without sacrificing the muscular performance RAM trucks are famous for. Paired with a smooth-shifting transmission and a refined 4x2 drivetrain, it offers a composed, quiet, and capable ride whether you are navigating city streets or cruising the open highway.
Inside, the sophisticated Black interior is packed with premium upgrades designed to elevate your driving experience. This model features the highly desirable Big Horn Level 2 Equipment Group, which seamlessly integrates convenience and comfort throughout the cabin. At the center of it all is the premium Radio: Uconnect 12 with Navigation, boasting a massive touchscreen display for effortless connectivity, paired with a high-performance audio system featuring 9 amplified speakers with a subwoofer. With its winning combination of V8 muscle, sleek Night Edition styling, and advanced cabin technology, this 2021 Ram 1500 Big Horn is ready to upgrade your driveway today.
