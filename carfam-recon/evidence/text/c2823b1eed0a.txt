Description
Experience the thrill of rally-bred performance with this pristine, Carfax 1-Owner 2022 Subaru WRX Limited. Draped in a striking Ceramic White exterior, this modern sport sedan perfectly blends aggressive styling with everyday usability. For driving enthusiasts who demand absolute control, the engaging manual transmission connects you directly to the road, making every commute or weekend run an unforgettable experience. Securing a coveted one-owner vehicle offers incredible peace of mind, ensuring this highly sought-after sports car has been exceptionally cared for from day one.
Under the hood, the 2022 Subaru WRX is powered by a responsive 2.4L 4-cylinder engine that delivers energetic power and immediate acceleration. Paired with Subaru's legendary Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive system, this vehicle offers unparalleled traction and cornering stability, giving you maximum control in all weather conditions. The precise manual transmission puts the power firmly in your hands, allowing you to fully exploit the capabilities of the chassis and powertrain with confidence.
Inside, the cabin of this Limited trim wraps you in comfort and style, featuring premium black ultrasuede upholstery accented by sporty red stitching. This vehicle comes equipped with thoughtful, protective enhancements designed to preserve its condition, including a door scuff protector, a rear seatback protector, alloy wheel locks, and a custom windshield sunshade to keep the interior cool on sunny days. Combining track-inspired engineering with everyday cabin refinement, this 2022 Subaru WRX Limited manual is the ultimate driver's car—ready for its next enthusiast owner.
