Description
The 2017 Toyota Sienna LE FWD 8-Passenger is an ideal family vehicle that blends practicality with style, featuring an eye-catching Predawn Gray Mica exterior. Under the hood, it boasts a robust 3.5L six-cylinder engine that provides a smooth and confident drive, perfect for both city commutes and long road trips. Its aerodynamic design, complemented by efficient 50 state emissions, ensures efficient fuel usage without compromising on performance.
Inside, the Sienna offers a spacious and comfortable environment with its sophisticated Dark Bisque interior. This model accommodates up to eight passengers, thanks to its versatile 3rd row seating, making it perfect for larger families or group outings. The rear A/C ensures that all passengers remain comfortable regardless of the temperature outside, while keyless entry adds an extra layer of convenience for busy parents on the go.
Entertainment and practicality are at the forefront of the Sienna's design, with Bluetooth connectivity allowing seamless integration of mobile devices for hands-free calls and music streaming. The vehicle's roof rack cross bars offer additional cargo space for weekend adventures or family vacations. Inside, every detail has been considered, right down to the carpet floor mats and door sill protectors, which safeguard against daily wear and tear. In essence, the 2017 Toyota Sienna LE FWD 8-Passenger successfully combines everyday functionality with elegance, making it a standout choice for any family.
