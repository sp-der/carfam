Description
Turn heads wherever you go in this stunning 2017 Hyundai Elantra SE. Finished in an eye-catching Scarlet Red Pearl exterior, this compact sedan perfectly balances modern styling with everyday practicality. As a highly desirable model from the Ulsan production facility, this vehicle offers a striking road presence and an exceptional blend of reliability and curb appeal, making it an outstanding choice for drivers who want to stand out from the crowd.
Under the hood, this 2017 Hyundai Elantra is powered by an efficient yet responsive 2.0L 4-cylinder engine. Paired with an engaging manual transmission, this powertrain puts the driver in complete control, offering a connected and spirited driving experience on both city streets and highways. Boasting PZEV (Partial Zero Emission Vehicle) credentials, this configuration delivers a smart, eco-conscious performance that maximizes efficiency without sacrificing the dependable capability you expect from a modern commuter sedan.
Inside, the cabin is designed with comfort and longevity in mind, featuring the inviting gray premium cloth seat trim that perfectly complements the bold exterior. The well-appointed interior provides a comfortable environment for every journey, ensuring you and your passengers travel in style. Blending a striking Scarlet Red Pearl finish, engaging manual performance, and a refined cabin, this 2017 Hyundai Elantra SE stands as a smart, stylish, and highly engaging choice for your next vehicle.
