Description
Discover the perfect blend of style, versatility, and modern sophistication with this stunning 2020 Chevrolet Equinox FWD 4dr LT w/1LT. Draped in an eye-catching Midnight Blue Metallic exterior paint that beautifully complements its refined Jet Black interior, this compact SUV commands attention on every road. Designed to elevate your daily commute and weekend getaways alike, this Equinox offers an ideal balance of curb appeal and functional design, making it a top choice for buyers seeking a reliable and attractive crossover.
Under the hood, this 2020 Chevrolet Equinox is powered by an efficient 1.5L 4-cylinder engine, delivering a responsive and smooth driving experience that makes navigating city streets and open highways effortless. The Front-Wheel Drive (FWD) system ensures confident handling and optimized fuel efficiency for your everyday adventures. To help preserve its sleek aesthetic during loading and unloading, this model includes the LPO rear bumper protector, adding a layer of practical utility to its sophisticated design, while the license plate front mounting package ensures clean integration.
Equipped with the Confidence & Convenience Package, this Equinox is loaded with premium amenities designed to maximize your comfort and peace of mind. Inside, you will enjoy the warmth of heated seats, dual-zone automatic climate control, and the seamless connectivity of the Chevrolet Infotainment 3 system featuring a 7-inch diagonal color touchscreen, AM/FM stereo, and Bluetooth. Modern convenience is at your fingertips with a power liftgate and remote start. Driving confidence is further elevated by an advanced suite of safety features, including a blind spot monitor, forward collision warning, parking assist, lane departure warning, and lane keep assist. Do not miss the opportunity to own this feature-packed 2020 Chevrolet Equinox—a smart, safe, and incredibly stylish SUV ready for its next journey.
