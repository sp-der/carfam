Description
Turn heads wherever you go with this striking 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 2WD Crew Cab 147" Custom. Draped in a clean, commanding Summit White exterior paint that contrasts beautifully with a refined Jet Black interior, this light-duty pickup offers an undeniable street presence. Built with the spacious Crew Cab configuration, it provides generous passenger legroom and everyday versatility, making it the perfect blend of a bold daily driver and a highly functional vehicle for buyers seeking both style and substance.
Under the hood, this 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 is powered by a dependable 4.3L 6-cylinder engine that delivers responsive performance and efficient power for your daily haul. Paired with a smooth-shifting drivetrain and a practical two-wheel-drive (2WD) setup, this truck offers comfortable handling on both city streets and open highways. It is fully prepared to tackle demanding tasks thanks to the included Trailering Package, giving you the enhanced towing capability and utility needed to confidently pull trailers, recreational toys, or work equipment.
This well-appointed pickup stands out from the crowd with a selection of premium option groups, including the Custom Value Package and the Custom Convenience Package, both designed to elevate your daily driving experience. The integrated Infotainment Package keeps you connected and entertained on the go, while the front of the vehicle gains an aggressive, modern touch with the distinctive LPO black bowtie emblem. Do not miss the opportunity to own this highly capable and feature-rich 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500—contact us today to schedule your test drive.
