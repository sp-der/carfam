Description
Dominating the road with an aggressive stance and sleek styling, this 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 4WD Crew Cab 147" LT Trail Boss is the ultimate blend of rugged capability and refined design. Shrouded in a commanding Black exterior that perfectly complements its bold LT Trail Boss styling, this truck commands attention on any terrain. Inside, the Jet Black interior offers a cohesive, sophisticated cabin environment designed for drivers who demand both toughness and modern style from their pickup.
Beneath the hood of this formidable 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 lies the powerful 5.3L 8-cylinder engine, delivering the robust horsepower and torque needed for dependable towing, hauling, and daily driving. Paired with a capable Four Wheel Drive (4WD) system, this LT Trail Boss is built to handle challenging terrain, off-road trails, and inclement weather with absolute confidence. The combination of its legendary V8 powertrain and truck utility ensures that you have the performance required for both demanding workdays and weekend getaways.
This exceptional truck comes equipped with packages that elevate comfort, utility, and peace of mind. The cabin features the Leather Package alongside the Convenience Package with bucket seats, offering premium seating surfaces and a driver-focused layout, while the Convenience Package II adds advanced convenience features to your daily drive. For enhanced driver awareness, the integrated Safety Package provides peace of mind on every journey, while the 4" black round LPO assist steps make entering and exiting the spacious Crew Cab effortless. Do not miss the opportunity to own this highly capable and heavily equipped 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 LT Trail Boss—a truck that truly refuses to compromise.
