Description
Discover the perfect blend of modern luxury and eco-conscious engineering with this pristine, Carfax 1-Owner 2023 Mercedes-Benz EQB 300 4MATIC SUV. Finished in a striking Polar White exterior with a sophisticated Black interior, this luxury electric vehicle offers the peace of mind that comes with a clean, single-owner history. As a premier all-electric SUV, it represents the future of premium family transportation, combining the prestigious Mercedes-Benz heritage with zero-emission efficiency.
Engineered for confident year-round driving, this 2023 Mercedes-Benz EQB 300 features an advanced All Wheel Drive (4MATIC) system that delivers exceptional traction and stability in varying road conditions. Powered by a fully electric powertrain, this SUV offers quiet, seamless acceleration and responsive handling without the need for a traditional gasoline engine. Whether navigating tight city streets or embarking on highway journeys, the EQB 300 4MATIC SUV provides a smooth, capable, and highly refined driving experience that redefines electric mobility.
This exceptional electric SUV comes well-equipped with practical and stylish features designed to enhance your daily drive. It includes a sleek rear spoiler for a sporty aesthetic touch, a convenient integrated garage door opener, and an essential charging cable to keep you powered up. Inside, the cabin is protected by a premium 4-piece set of all-season floor mats, while wheel locking bolts offer added security for the exterior. Do not miss the opportunity to own this highly desirable, single-owner 2023 Mercedes-Benz EQB 300 4MATIC SUV—a sophisticated and versatile choice for drivers ready to transition to premium electric power.
