Description
Discover the perfect blend of sophistication and everyday utility with this stunning 2020 Toyota Highlander XLE FWD. Finished in the highly desirable Blizzard Pearl, a premium special color, this midsize SUV commands attention on the road while offering a refined, modern aesthetic. Inside, you are greeted by a sleek, premium Black interior that balances comfort and durability, making it an ideal choice for daily commutes and family adventures alike.
Under the hood, this 2020 Toyota Highlander XLE FWD is powered by a robust 3.5L 6-cylinder engine, delivering the smooth acceleration and confident power needed for highway cruising and city driving alike. Engineered with versatile capability, this front-wheel-drive SUV features 50 State Emissions compliance and is fully equipped for your next journey. Practical utility features include cargo cross bars (TMS) perfect for mounting extra gear, alongside an all-weather floor liner and cargo tray (TMS) to protect the cabin from dirt, mud, and spills.
Inside, the cabin of this XLE trim elevates your driving experience with advanced connectivity and high-end convenience. Stay seamlessly connected and on track with the integrated Radio: Premium Audio with Navigation, ensuring your favorite entertainment and clear route guidance are always at your fingertips. Combining legendary reliability with standout styling and adventure-ready utility, this 2020 Toyota Highlander XLE FWD is the complete package for buyers seeking a premium, dependable SUV.
