Description
The 2021 Toyota Corolla Hybrid LE CVT in Black Sand Pearl presents a striking blend of style and efficiency in a compact sedan package. Its sleek exterior is complemented by LED headlights, designed for both enhanced visibility and energy efficiency. The sporty allure of its black-clad interior offers modern sophistication with comfortable seating and intuitive controls, perfect for any urban driver.
Under the hood, the Corolla is powered by a 1.8L 4-cylinder engine, offering an exceptional hybrid performance that maximizes fuel efficiency without compromising on power. The advanced active cruise control and forward collision warning provide an extra layer of safety, allowing for a more relaxed and confident driving experience. Additionally, the lane departure warning feature prevents unintended lane shifts, enhancing road security on longer drives.
Modern convenience is well-accounted for with features like Bluetooth connectivity and keyless entry, ensuring seamless integration and easy access to essential controls. The comprehensive safety package, including a backup camera, ensures peace of mind on the road. Thoughtful additions such as the carpet mat package, door edge guards, and rear bumper protector provide functional enhancements, protecting your investment. With alloy wheel locks, a cargo-net envelope, and telematics, the Corolla Hybrid LE CVT underscores Toyota's dedication to innovative and reliable automobile excellence.
