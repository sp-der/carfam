Description
Turn heads wherever you go in the stunning 2024 Kia K5 GT-Line Auto FWD. Dressed in the highly sought-after Wolf Gray exterior paint, this midsize sport sedan perfectly blends modern styling with an aggressive, road-ready stance. The bold design of the GT-Line trim ensures you make a powerful first impression, whether you are commuting to work or heading out for a weekend getaway. It is the ideal choice for drivers who demand a vehicle that looks as sophisticated as it feels.
Under the hood, the 2024 Kia K5 GT-Line Auto FWD is powered by an efficient and responsive 1.6L 4-cylinder engine paired with a smooth automatic transmission. This front-wheel-drive powertrain delivers a great balance of spirited acceleration and daily fuel efficiency, making every drive both engaging and practical. Engineered for confident handling and a composed ride, this sedan offers a dynamic driving experience that easily tames winding roads and highway commutes alike.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a comfortable and premium environment featuring black cloth and SynTex seat trim alongside matching carpeted floor mats. Elevating your driving experience are the highly desirable GT-Line Panoramic Sunroof Package, which floods the cabin with natural light, and the GT-Line Premium Package, which adds enhanced convenience to your daily drive. Do not miss your chance to own this exceptionally styled 2024 Kia K5 GT-Line Auto FWD—schedule your test drive today and experience its perfect blend of comfort and curb appeal firsthand.
