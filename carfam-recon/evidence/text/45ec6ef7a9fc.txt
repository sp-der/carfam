Description
Discover the perfect blend of modern style, efficiency, and reliability with this stunning 2013 Hyundai Sonata 4dr Sdn 2.4L Auto GLS PZEV. Dressed in an elegant Harbor Gray Metallic exterior, this midsize sedan stands out on the road with its sweeping, aerodynamic profile, making it an exceptional choice for daily commuters and families alike. Combining sophisticated curb appeal with practical everyday usability, this Sonata GLS delivers a premium driving experience that captures attention while remaining highly functional.
Under the hood, this sedan is powered by a responsive 2.4L 4-cylinder engine paired with a smooth-shifting automatic transmission. As a Partial Zero Emission Vehicle (PZEV), this powertrain is engineered to minimize environmental impact while maximizing fuel efficiency, making it as gentle on your wallet as it is on the environment. Whether navigating tight city streets or cruising on the open highway, the front-wheel-drive dynamics of the 2013 Hyundai Sonata ensure a composed, quiet, and dependable ride every time you get behind the wheel.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with comfortable gray cloth seats that perfectly complement the vehicle's refined color scheme. This model is thoughtfully equipped with practical features to enhance your daily drive, including a convenient remote start system, protective carpeted floor mats, and a trunk-mounted cargo net to keep your belongings secure. On the outside, a rear bumper applique adds a layer of protection against minor scuffs during loading and unloading. Combining comfort, utility, and efficient performance, this 2013 Hyundai Sonata is ready to elevate your daily drive—contact us today to schedule your test drive.
