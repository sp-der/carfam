Description
The 2018 GMC Sierra 1500 4WD Crew Cab 143.5" SLT in Onyx Black is a striking combination of power and luxury, ideal for both rugged terrains and urban streets. Its sleek exterior is complemented by 20" x 9" polished aluminum wheels and chromed tubular 6" rectangular assist steps, giving it a commanding presence. The interior, adorned in Jet Black leather, offers both style and comfort with features like heated seats and auto climate control.
This model is powered by a robust 5.3L V8 engine, paired with a Four Wheel Drive drivetrain, ensuring optimal performance and control in all driving conditions. The truck’s advanced technology is highlighted by a state-of-the-art infotainment system featuring an 8" diagonal color touch screen with navigation, AM/FM/SiriusXM HD Radio, and Bluetooth connectivity. The premium Bose sound system with six speakers delivers an unrivaled audio experience, making every drive enjoyable.
For added convenience and safety, the Sierra 1500 includes remote start, a backup camera, and the SLT Premium Package. Its towing package makes it a perfect companion for adventures, whether you’re hauling equipment or taking a family trip. The lighting package further enhances visibility, ensuring optimal safety in low-light conditions. This vehicle is a remarkable blend of capability, comfort, and style, catering to those who demand excellence on the road.
