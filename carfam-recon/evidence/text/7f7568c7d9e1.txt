Description
Discover the perfect blend of sophistication and family-friendly versatility with this stunning 2016 Toyota Highlander AWD 4dr V6 Limited. Draped in a sleek Silver Sky Metallic exterior that beautifully complements its refined Ash interior, this premium SUV stands out in any setting. As the highly sought-after Limited trim, it delivers an upscale driving experience designed for buyers who refuse to compromise on style, cabin comfort, or daily practicality.
Under the hood, this 2016 Toyota Highlander is powered by a robust 3.5L 6-cylinder engine, offering the perfect balance of smooth acceleration and dependable power. Paired with an advanced All Wheel Drive (AWD) drivetrain, this vehicle provides exceptional traction and confidence-inspiring stability across various road conditions and seasonal weather. Whether you are navigating daily highway commutes or embarking on a weekend family getaway, the capable powertrain ensures a secure, quiet, and engaging ride.
This meticulously equipped Limited model comes enhanced with excellent utility features, including sturdy running boards for easy cabin entry and roof rack cross bars ready to carry your extra gear. Inside, you will enjoy the added convenience of the preferred accessory package alongside a retractable cargo cover to keep your belongings secure and out of sight. Fully compliant with 50 state emissions, this versatile 2016 Toyota Highlander AWD 4dr V6 Limited represents an exceptional opportunity for buyers seeking a reliable, feature-rich, and stylish adventure partner.
