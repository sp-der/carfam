Description
Get ready to dominate both the highway and the trails in this striking 2019 Ram 1500 Rebel 4x4 Crew Cab. Finished in a sophisticated Granite Crystal Metallic Clearcoat, this truck commands an undeniable presence on the road. Inside, the bold Red/Black interior perfectly complements the truck's aggressive personality, offering a premium and sporty cabin experience. Designed for those who demand both rugged attitude and refined styling, this pickup stands out in any crowd, making it the ultimate choice for truck enthusiasts seeking a vehicle that looks as powerful as it performs.
Under the hood, this capable truck is powered by the robust 5.7L V8 HEMI MDS VVT eTorque engine, which seamlessly blends legendary V8 capability with mild-hybrid technology for optimized performance. Paired with an advanced four-wheel drive system, the 2019 Ram 1500 Rebel is engineered to conquer challenging off-road terrain and demanding towing tasks with ease. The spacious Crew Cab body style ensures plenty of legroom for passengers, while the practical 5'7" box provides ample utility and cargo space for your gear, tools, or weekend toys.
Equipped with the Rebel Level 2 Equipment Group, this truck is loaded with premium convenience and technology features that elevate every drive. Enjoy a concert-like audio experience on the go with the 9 amplified speakers with a subwoofer, while advanced safety tech like Blind Spot & Cross Path Detection and ParkSense Front/Rear Park Assist with Stop provide added confidence in tight spots and heavy traffic. Delivering the perfect mix of raw power, modern safety, and everyday comfort, this 2019 Ram 1500 Rebel 4x4 Crew Cab is ready for your next great adventure.
