Description
Ready to conquer both the daily commute and the rugged backcountry, this stunning 2023 Toyota Tacoma 4WD TRD Off Road Double Cab 5' Bed V6 AT is the ultimate midsize truck for adventure seekers. Clad in the highly sought-after Lunar Rock exterior color, this Tacoma immediately stands out with its bold, modern aesthetic. The Double Cab configuration offers the perfect balance of passenger space and utility, ensuring you and your crew ride in style whether you are navigating city streets or hitting the dirt trails.
Under the hood, this powerhouse is driven by a robust 3.5L 6-cylinder engine paired with an automatic transmission, delivering the reliable performance and capability Toyota is famous for. Equipped with a true Four Wheel Drive (4WD) system and specialized TRD Off-Road tuning, this truck is engineered to tackle challenging terrains with absolute confidence. The versatile 5-foot bed provides ample cargo space for your gear, making it a highly capable partner for hauling, camping, or weekend projects.
Step inside to a refined and durable Cement/Black interior, which is well-protected by the included all-weather floor liners and door sill protectors. This vehicle is loaded with premium upgrades, including 5-inch black oval tube steps for easy cabin access, a convenient bed light to illuminate your gear, and the striking Black Out Package that adds a sleek, aggressive touch. Nighttime visibility is also enhanced with high-performance LED headlights featuring black bezels and matching LED fog lights. With its unmatched capability, distinctive styling, and legendary reliability, this 2023 Toyota Tacoma is fully equipped and waiting for its next great journey.
