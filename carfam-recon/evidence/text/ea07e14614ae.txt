Description
Get ready to elevate your daily drive with this stunning 2022 Toyota Corolla SE Manual. Finished in a crisp, sophisticated White exterior that perfectly contrasts with its refined Black interior, this sporty compact sedan is designed for drivers who crave a more connected and engaging experience on the road. Combining Toyota's legendary reliability with a modern, aggressive aesthetic, this eye-catching Corolla SE stands out from the crowd, making it the perfect choice for buyers seeking both style and substance in a modern commuter car.
Under the hood, the 2022 Toyota Corolla SE is powered by a spirited 2.0L 4-cylinder engine, delivering a responsive and efficient performance that brings excitement to every drive. Paired with an engaging manual transmission, this powertrain puts total control back into your hands, offering crisp shifts and an athletic driving dynamic that automatic alternatives simply cannot replicate. Whether you are navigating tight city corners or merging onto the highway, the sport-tuned suspension and driver-centric setup of this Corolla ensure a rewarding ride every time you slip behind the wheel.
Beyond its stellar performance, this vehicle comes equipped with selected features that enhance both its styling and everyday utility. The athletic exterior is elevated by a sleek rear spoiler (tms) and distinct blackout emblem overlays (tms), giving it a custom, aggressive look. Practicality is also front and center, featuring protective door edge guards (tms) and a rear bumper protector (tms) to guard against daily wear and tear, while the cabin remains clean and inviting thanks to the included carpet mat package (tms). Combining athletic styling, engaging performance, and thoughtful protection, this 2022 Toyota Corolla SE Manual is a rare find that is ready for its next owner.
