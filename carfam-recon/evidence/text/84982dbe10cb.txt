Description
Command the road in style with this jaw-dropping 2019 Ram 1500 Rebel 4x2 Crew Cab. Cloaked in a menacing Diamond Black Crystal Pearlcoat exterior, this aggressive light-duty pickup truck combines rugged off-road styling with premium street appeal. Step inside the spacious cabin to find a bold, highly detailed Red/Black interior that perfectly balances athletic design with daily durability. Whether you are navigating city streets or heading out on a weekend adventure, this truck makes a powerful first impression from every angle.
Under the hood lies the legendary 5.7L V8 HEMI MDS VVT eTorque engine, delivering exceptional horsepower and modern mild-hybrid capability for responsive performance. This 2019 Ram 1500 Rebel is built to work, equipped with a dedicated towing pkg and the Trailer Tow Group to ensure you can haul trailers, boats, or powersports equipment with absolute confidence. The 5'7" utility box is highly protected and ready for action thanks to a durable spray-in bedliner, making it easy to load up cargo without worrying about scratching the truck's bed.
Premium convenience and advanced safety technology define the driving experience, elevated by the Rebel Level 1 Equipment Group. Stay comfortable in any weather with cozy heated seats, and stay connected on the go with hands-free Bluetooth wireless technology. Parking and maneuvering in tight spaces are made effortless with the integrated backup camera, parking assist, and ParkSense front/rear park assist w/stop. Rounding out this impressive package is a premium lighting pkg featuring high-visibility LED headlights, auto high beams, and keyless entry for seamless daily operations. This stunning truck offers the perfect mix of V8 capability, modern comfort, and distinctive style.
