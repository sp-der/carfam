Description
Experience the perfect blend of sophistication and modern styling with this stunning 2014 Toyota Avalon XLE Touring. Cloaked in the highly desirable Blizzard Pearl special color, this elegant sedan commands attention on any road with its sleek profile and athletic stance. Inside, the refined cabin welcomes you with a pristine Light Gray interior, offering an inviting and upscale atmosphere designed for drivers who appreciate both aesthetic appeal and everyday comfort.
Beneath its sculpted hood lies a potent 3.5L 6-cylinder engine, delivering seamless acceleration and dependable power that makes highway cruising and daily commuting effortless. The 2014 Toyota Avalon is renowned for its refined driving dynamics, and this XLE Touring trim offers a balanced, comfortable ride that handles curves with confidence while maintaining exceptional cabin tranquility. Engineered with 50 state emissions compliance, this powertrain ensures a highly efficient yet engaging performance that aligns with modern standards without sacrificing driving excitement.
This vehicle is equipped with key features designed to elevate your peace of mind and protect your investment, including a Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross Traffic Alert to enhance your situational awareness on busy roads. The exterior's pristine finish is protected by a functional rear bumper applique, while the cabin and cargo areas remain immaculate thanks to the included carpet floor mats and matching carpet trunk mat. Whether you are seeking a reliable commuter or a comfortable long-distance cruiser, this 2014 Toyota Avalon XLE Touring represents an outstanding opportunity to own a premium sedan that delivers on safety, performance, and style.
