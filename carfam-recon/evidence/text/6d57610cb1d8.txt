Description
Experience the perfect blend of elegance and versatility with this stunning 2013 Lexus RX 350 FWD 4dr. Draped in a sophisticated Obsidian exterior, this luxury crossover commands attention with its sleek profile and executive road presence. Step inside to find a refined Black interior that provides a quiet, welcoming sanctuary from the hustle and bustle of daily life. As one of the most trusted names in the luxury SUV segment, this vehicle represents an outstanding opportunity for buyers seeking a premium, reliable, and stylish ride that elevates every journey.
Under the hood, the 2013 Lexus RX 350 is powered by a robust and smooth 3.5L 6-cylinder engine, delivering effortless acceleration and the refined performance Lexus is famous for. The front-wheel-drive (FWD) configuration offers balanced handling and a composed ride quality, making it ideal for both daily city commuting and long-distance highway cruising. This luxury crossover seamlessly combines powertrain efficiency with daily utility, offering a spacious four-door layout with a generous cargo area that easily adapts to your active lifestyle.
This exceptional vehicle comes loaded with desirable upgrades, including the Comfort Package, Premium Package, and a dedicated Navigation Package to guide you on every adventure. The meticulously crafted cabin features a wood and leather-wrapped steering wheel and shift knob, alongside modern conveniences like keyless entry, telematics, Bluetooth connectivity, a premium sound system, and rear a/c for passenger comfort. Parking and loading are made effortless thanks to the intuitive parking assist and a convenient power liftgate. Delivering an unmatched combination of comfort, prestige, and advanced features, this 2013 Lexus RX 350 is ready to deliver a first-class driving experience to its next owner.
