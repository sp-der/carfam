Description
Experience the perfect blend of athletic styling and executive luxury with this stunning 2017 Lexus IS Turbo. Finished in the highly sought-after Eminent White Pearl exterior paint, this premium sport sedan commands attention on every road. Step inside the cabin to find a sophisticated, driver-focused environment featuring a striking Black interior beautifully accented by Illustrious Piano Black trim. Designed for buyers who demand both sophisticated elegance and a commanding road presence, this vehicle represents the pinnacle of modern styling and comfort.
Beneath the sculpted hood of this 2017 Lexus IS Turbo RWD lies a responsive 2.0L 4-cylinder turbocharged engine, engineered to deliver an engaging and spirited driving experience. Sending power directly to the rear wheels, this rear-wheel-drive (RWD) configuration offers the classic, balanced handling dynamics that driving enthusiasts crave. Whether you are navigating tight city corners or cruising on the open highway, the refined powertrain offers a seamless blend of performance and efficiency, making every drive feel effortlessly rewarding.
This exceptional sedan comes equipped with a select suite of high-value features designed to elevate your daily commute. Find your destination with ease using the integrated navigation system package, while the premium package adds an extra layer of refinement to the overall cabin experience. Driving confidence is enhanced by advanced driver assistance features, including a blind spot monitor with rear cross traffic alert and intuitive park assist to make parking and lane changes seamless. Complete with a practical cargo net to keep your gear secure in the trunk, this 2017 Lexus IS Turbo is the complete package of technology, safety, and luxury.
