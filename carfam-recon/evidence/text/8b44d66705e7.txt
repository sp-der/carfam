Description
Turn heads wherever you go in this stunning 2017 Toyota Corolla iM CVT (Natl). Finished in an eye-catching Electric Storm Blue exterior, this sporty hatchback seamlessly blends aggressive, modern styling with everyday hatchback practicality. Step inside to find a sleek, comfortable Black interior designed to elevate your daily commute or weekend road trips. Perfect for buyers seeking a reliable yet distinctively styled compact car, this Corolla iM offers a unique personality that stands out from the crowd while delivering the legendary quality and peace of mind synonymous with the Toyota brand.
Under the hood, the 2017 Toyota Corolla iM is powered by an efficient 1.8L 4-cylinder engine paired with a smooth and responsive Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT). This powertrain combination delivers a highly composed ride and excellent fuel economy, making it an ideal choice for navigating tight city streets or cruising down the open highway. Designed to be environmentally conscious, this vehicle is equipped with 50 state emissions certification, ensuring clean performance wherever your travels take you.
This specific model comes equipped with thoughtful, practical additions that enhance both style and utility. A sporty rear wind deflector complements the hatchback's aerodynamic lines, while the included carpet mat package helps keep the Black cabin looking pristine. Managing groceries or gear is made easy with the convenient envelope cargo net, designed to secure your loose items while on the move. Boasting a striking Electric Storm Blue paint job and smart everyday features, this versatile hatchback is the perfect upgrade for buyers demanding efficiency, style, and utility.
