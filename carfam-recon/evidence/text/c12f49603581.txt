Description
Discover the perfect blend of premium passenger transport and legendary capability with this Carfax 1-Owner 2016 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Passenger Van RWD 2500 144". Finished in a sophisticated Brilliant Silver Metallic exterior paired with a refined Black interior, this luxury transport vehicle immediately commands attention on the road. Whether you are managing a commercial fleet, operating a premium shuttle service, or seeking a spacious group hauler, this exceptionally maintained, single-owner Sprinter offers the prestige, safety, and reliability that only a Mercedes-Benz can deliver.
Engineered to deliver dependable performance and efficiency, this RWD 2500 144" model is powered by a responsive 2.1L 4-cylinder engine. The rear-wheel-drive configuration ensures confident handling and traction, providing a smooth and stable ride even when carrying a full cabin. Its nimble 144-inch wheelbase strikes the ideal balance between generous interior passenger volume and daily drivability, making it easy to navigate tight city streets while remaining composed and effortless on the open highway.
Inside, passenger accommodation is elevated to a first-class experience. This 2016 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter comes equipped with the rear passenger comfort package, ensuring your guests travel in ultimate relaxation. To conquer cold weather, the cabin features both an auxiliary rear heater and a high-performance 17k BTU fuel-fired pre-heater/booster to keep the entire interior perfectly climate-controlled. Convenient touches like a designated cupholder in the passenger compartment complete this thoughtfully designed layout, making this highly capable, single-owner Mercedes-Benz passenger van an outstanding investment for discerning buyers.
