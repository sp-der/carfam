Description
Ready to elevate your business's mobile operations? This 2020 Ford Transit Cargo Van T-350 130" Med Rf 9500 GVWR RWD is the ultimate workhorse designed to maximize productivity. Finished in a professional Oxford White exterior, this commercial vehicle features a highly versatile medium roof configuration paired with a maneuverable 130-inch wheelbase. Whether you are navigating tight city streets or setting up a custom mobile workstation, this Ford Transit provides the ideal foundation for contractors, delivery fleets, and tradespeople looking for a reliable, professional-grade cargo solution.
Under the hood, this rear-wheel-drive cargo van delivers exceptional capability thanks to its powerful 3.5L EcoBoost V6 engine, offering the perfect balance of performance and torque for demanding jobs. Built to handle heavy-duty hauling, the 2020 Ford Transit T-350 boasts a robust 9,500-pound Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) to accommodate substantial payloads. Capability and durability are further enhanced by a heavy-duty front axle and a 3.73 limited-slip axle ratio, ensuring confident traction and steady power delivery even when fully loaded or traversing challenging job sites.
Inside, you will find a functional cabin styled in Dark Palazzo Grey, designed to keep you focused and comfortable throughout the workday. This specific model is uniquely optimized for custom upfitting and demanding electrical needs, featuring high-output dual alternators rated at 250 amps each alongside an auxiliary fuse panel to easily power your onboard tools, electronics, or specialized mobile equipment. Combining dependable Ford engineering with these high-value utility upgrades, this 2020 Ford Transit Cargo Van is ready to put in the work and drive your business forward.
