Description
The 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 2WD Double Cab 143.5" LT w/1LT stands out as a premier choice for truck buyers seeking a blend of commanding style, daily dependability, and versatile utility. Finished in a sleek, sophisticated Black exterior paint that perfectly complements its refined Jet Black interior, this truck commands attention on any road. The Double Cab configuration offers the perfect balance of passenger comfort and cargo flexibility, making this legendary light-duty pickup an ideal fit for both active families and hard-working professionals who demand presence and practicality in one package.
Under the hood, this 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 delivers formidable performance thanks to its 5.3L EcoTec3 V8 engine. Engineered with advanced technologies like Active Fuel Management, direct injection, and variable valve timing, this engine optimizes power delivery without sacrificing efficiency. Ready to tackle demanding tasks, this 2WD truck comes equipped with a heavy-duty locking rear differential for enhanced traction and control, alongside a robust Trailering Package. Whether you are pulling a trailer, hauling gear in the bed, or navigating your daily commute, this powertrain provides the reliable muscle and smooth handling you expect from a Chevy truck.
Inside the quiet and well-appointed cabin, comfort meets outstanding utility, highlighted by the popular All Star Edition package which adds an extra layer of convenience and style. Staying organized on the go is simple with the dealer-installed LPO rear under-seat composite storage bin, keeping your essential tools and valuables secure and out of sight. Bringing together strong V8 capability, smart storage solutions, and a timeless design, this 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 LT is a well-rounded vehicle ready to elevate your driving experience and handle your next adventure with ease.
