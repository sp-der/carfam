Description
Experience the pinnacle of full-size luxury with this stunning 2015 Cadillac Escalade ESV 4WD 4dr Premium. Draped in the exquisite White Diamond Tricoat exterior paint, this premium extended-length SUV commands attention on every road. Inside, the cabin is a sanctuary of sophisticated design, featuring the rich and distinctive Kona Brown w/Jet Black Accents interior color scheme. Perfectly blending bold styling with unparalleled road presence, this luxury vehicle is the ultimate statement piece for buyers seeking first-class travel.
Beneath the sculpted hood lies a formidable 6.2L 8 Cylinders engine that delivers effortless power and authoritative capability. Paired with a robust Four Wheel Drive drivetrain, this 2015 Cadillac Escalade ESV provides confident traction and control across a variety of driving conditions. The extended ESV body style maximizes cargo utility and passenger comfort, making it an exceptionally capable vehicle that handles family road trips and daily commutes with ease.
This premium model is equipped with refined comfort features, including the Kona Brown w/Jet Black Accents full leather seats with mini perforated inserts that offer exquisite support. Entry and exit are made effortless and elegant thanks to the assist steps power-retractable running side board with led lighting. Complete with a front black license plate bracket and the 2015 interim processing code, this SUV represents the peak of utility and luxury. Do not miss the opportunity to own this iconic 2015 Cadillac Escalade ESV.
