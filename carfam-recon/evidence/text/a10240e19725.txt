Description
Elevate your daily drive with this sophisticated 2017 INFINITI Q50 2.0t RWD, a luxury sport sedan that perfectly balances modern elegance with athletic performance. Draped in a striking Pure White exterior, this premium vehicle commands attention on the road while offering a clean, upscale aesthetic. Step inside the cabin to find an inviting and meticulously crafted environment featuring a refined Graphite interior, highlighted by comfortable leather seats and graphite premium leatherette seating surfaces that deliver both style and comfort.
Under the hood, this 2017 INFINITI Q50 is powered by a responsive 2.0L 4-cylinder engine that delivers spirited acceleration and a highly engaging driving experience. Channeling its power through a classic rear-wheel-drive (RWD) drivetrain, this sedan offers the dynamic handling and precise cornering capability that driving enthusiasts crave. Additionally, this vehicle comes equipped with [c03] 50 state emissions, ensuring efficient and compliant performance across the country.
Inside and out, this luxury sedan is loaded with advanced technology and premium amenities designed to enhance your comfort and safety. Enjoy the convenience of keyless entry, multi-zone climate control, and Bluetooth wireless connectivity, alongside the upscale [j01] moonroof package with a built-in moonroof. Driving is made effortless with a high-definition backup camera, rain-sensing wipers, LED headlights, auto high beams, and an enhanced lighting package, while the [s55] literature kit and [z66] activation disclaimer complete the vehicle's documentation. Do not miss your chance to own this exceptional 2017 INFINITI Q50 2.0t RWD—a perfect blend of luxury and performance.
