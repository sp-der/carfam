Description
Experience the perfect blend of executive sophistication and athletic styling with this stunning 2017 BMW 5 Series. Finished in an elegant Dark Graphite Metallic, this 540i xDrive Sedan commands attention on any road, offering a sleek, modern profile that epitomizes premium German engineering. As a benchmark in the midsize luxury class, it delivers a commanding presence, making it the ideal choice for discerning buyers who refuse to compromise on style or prestige.
Under the hood, this 2017 BMW 5 Series 540i xDrive Sedan is powered by a potent 3.0L 6 Cylinders engine that provides effortless acceleration and refined power delivery. Paired with an advanced All Wheel Drive system, this sedan ensures exceptional traction, stability, and handling confidence across a variety of road conditions and seasons. Whether you are navigating spirited winding roads or embarking on a long highway journey, the capable powertrain and drivetrain setup offer a smooth, responsive, and dynamic driving experience.
Inside, luxury takes center stage with a beautifully appointed cabin featuring gorgeous Ivory White Dakota leather upholstery. This striking interior color contrast beautifully complements the Dark Graphite Metallic exterior, creating an inviting and upscale atmosphere for both driver and passengers. Impeccable craftsmanship and premium materials ensure that every journey in this luxury sedan is as comfortable as it is memorable, making this vehicle a truly complete package for anyone seeking top-tier comfort and performance.
