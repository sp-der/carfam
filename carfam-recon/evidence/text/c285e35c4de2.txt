Description
Get ready to conquer any terrain in style with this head-turning 2019 Jeep Wrangler Rubicon 4x4. Finished in the highly sought-after Sting-Gray Clearcoat exterior paint, this iconic off-roader perfectly blends modern ruggedness with classic heritage design. Whether you are navigating your daily commute or seeking weekend adventures off the beaten path, this Rubicon is built to deliver the ultimate open-air freedom and commanding road presence that SUV buyers crave.
Under the hood, this trail-ready machine is powered by an efficient and responsive 2.0L 4-cylinder engine, seamlessly paired with a smooth-shifting 8-speed automatic (850re) transmission. Engineered for maximum capability, the robust four-wheel-drive system ensures exceptional traction through mud, snow, and rocks. Adding to its rugged utility and aggressive styling is the factory-equipped steel bumper group, providing heavy-duty front and rear protection designed to withstand the toughest trail obstacles.
Step inside the cabin to find a refined Black interior designed to withstand the elements while keeping you connected. This 2019 Jeep Wrangler Rubicon comes heavily equipped with the desirable 8.4" radio & premium audio group, featuring an immersive Alpine premium audio system that delivers crystal-clear sound on every journey. Completing this premium package is the versatile black 3-piece hard top, offering excellent cabin insulation when on the highway and easy removal for those days when only a true open-air experience will do.
