Experience the perfect blend of stealthy styling and exclusive design with the 2022 Hyundai Sonata N Line Night Edition 2.5T. This limited-availability (*Ltd Avail*) sports sedan commands immediate attention on the road, showcasing a brilliant Quartz White exterior finish that beautifully contrasts with its dark, aggressive Night Edition accents. Designed for drivers who demand both individuality and a premium road presence, this rare model offers a striking aesthetic that stands out in any setting.
Beneath its sculpted hood lies a high-performance 2.5L 4 Cylinders engine, delivering the turbocharged power and athletic driving dynamics that define the N Line pedigree. This sophisticated powertrain offers rapid acceleration and highly responsive handling, transforming your daily commute into an engaging driving experience. Whether you are navigating tight city corners or cruising on the open highway, the 2022 Hyundai Sonata N Line Night Edition 2.5T provides a superb balance of refined road manners and thrilling performance.
Step inside the cabin to discover a driver-focused Black interior anchored by premium black nappa leather seating surfaces that elevate comfort and support on every journey. This vehicle is equipped with thoughtful, practical additions designed to preserve its premium feel, including custom-fit carpeted floor mats, a reversible cargo tray for versatile trunk utility, and a handy first aid kit. Offering a rare combination of limited-edition style, robust performance, and upscale cabin features, this 2022 Hyundai Sonata N Line Night Edition 2.5T *Ltd Avail* is the ultimate upgrade for the discerning buyer.
