Description
Step into premium comfort and style with this stunning 2020 Kia Telluride SX AWD. Finished in the highly desirable Snow White Pearl exterior paint, this award-winning midsize SUV commands attention on any road while offering the perfect blend of family-friendly utility and upscale design. As the top-tier SX trim, this vehicle represents the pinnacle of the Telluride lineup, delivering an elevated driving experience that makes every daily commute or weekend getaway feel like a first-class journey.
Under the hood, this 2020 Kia Telluride is powered by a robust 3.8L 6-cylinder engine mated to an advanced All Wheel Drive system, providing confident traction and smooth power delivery in all driving conditions. Built for adventure, this model comes equipped with the factory towing package, including a tow hitch with a harness, allowing you to easily pull trailers, boats, or powersports equipment. Whether you are navigating snowy winter roads or heading out on a cross-country road trip, this SUV provides the rugged capability and dependable performance your lifestyle demands.
Inside, the cabin features a sophisticated Gray interior enhanced by the ultra-premium SX Prestige Package, offering an upscale atmosphere filled with high-end comfort and cutting-edge refinements. Carpeted floor mats are also included to keep the refined interior pristine. Offering the perfect combination of capability, luxury, and styling, this 2020 Kia Telluride SX AWD is the ultimate modern family cruiser that is ready for your next destination.
