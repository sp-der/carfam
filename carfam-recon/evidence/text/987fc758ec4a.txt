Description
Experience the pinnacle of premium automotive luxury with this stunning 2017 Cadillac Escalade ESV 4WD Platinum. Finished in a gorgeous Crystal White Tricoat, this extended-length luxury SUV commands attention on any road with its bold styling and sophisticated presence. As the ultimate expression of Cadillac’s flagship SUV, the Platinum trim delivers an unmatched combination of spaciousness, status, and refined craftsmanship for discerning buyers who refuse to compromise on quality or road presence.
Beneath its elegant exterior, this 2017 Cadillac Escalade ESV is engineered for effortless capability and confident performance. Powered by a robust 6.2L 8-cylinder engine, this luxury SUV delivers commanding acceleration and the raw power needed for highway cruising or towing. Coupled with an advanced Four Wheel Drive (4WD) system, it provides exceptional traction and control across various road conditions. The ESV designation ensures maximum utility, offering an elongated wheelbase that dramatically expands interior cargo capacity and passenger space, making it the ideal vehicle for long-distance family travel.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a rich Tuscan Brown interior that defines first-class comfort. This premium SUV is equipped with thoughtful, protective, and functional upgrades, including the LPO interior protection package featuring LPO all-weather floor mats for the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd rows to keep the footwells pristine. Additional utility is provided by the integrated LPO black roof rack rails for carrying extra gear, while the LPO cargo shade keeps your belongings secure and out of sight in the rear. With its perfect blend of elite styling, robust V8 power, and practical enhancements, this 2017 Cadillac Escalade ESV Platinum stands as the ultimate luxury sanctuary on wheels.
