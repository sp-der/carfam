Description
Experience the perfect blend of athletic performance and modern luxury with this stunning 2018 INFINITI Q60 RED SPORT 400 RWD. Wrapped in a sophisticated Graphite Shadow exterior, this premium sports coupe makes an unforgettable first impression on the road. The sleek, aerodynamic exterior contours transition seamlessly to a jaw-dropping, driver-focused cabin finished in a vibrant Monaco Red interior. This striking color combination elevates the vehicle’s aggressive styling, making it an ideal choice for buyers who demand both aesthetic distinction and head-turning road presence.
Under the hood lies the heart of a true performance machine: a potent 3.0L 6-cylinder engine that delivers an exhilarating 400 horsepower. Paired with a dynamic Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) configuration, this 2018 INFINITI Q60 RED SPORT 400 offers precise handling, responsive acceleration, and an engaging driving experience that connects you directly to the pavement. Engineered for sports-car enthusiasts, the twin-turbocharged powertrain combines effortless high-speed cruising with nimble cornering capabilities, ensuring every drive is as thrilling as the last.
This meticulously equipped luxury coupe is loaded with premium options designed to enhance your daily commute and ownership experience. Step inside to be greeted by the elegant glow of the [n92] illuminated kick plates, while the [p03] sensory package 3.0t sport elevates cabin comfort, entertainment, and convenience to a higher standard. Practicality is also prioritized with the [m92] cargo package and the included [s55] literature kit, while the vehicle remains fully compliant across the country thanks to [c03] 50 state emissions. Offering a rare combination of raw twin-turbo power, striking aesthetics, and high-end features, this 2018 INFINITI Q60 RED SPORT 400 RWD is the ultimate luxury sports coupe for those who refuse to compromise.
