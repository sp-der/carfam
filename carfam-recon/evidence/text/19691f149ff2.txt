Description
Experience the perfect blend of American luxury and modern sophistication with this pristine, Carfax 1-Owner 2022 Chrysler 300 Touring L RWD. Finished in a stunning Bright White Clearcoat, this full-size sedan commands attention on the road with its bold, sculptural lines and iconic street presence. Purchasing a Carfax 1-Owner vehicle offers incredible peace of mind, ensuring this premium sedan has been cared for from day one. Whether you are commuting in style or embarking on a long highway cruise, this Chrysler 300 delivers a commanding aesthetic that stands out in any setting.
Under the hood, this 2022 Chrysler 300 Touring L RWD is powered by the dependable 3.6L V6 24V VVT engine, delivering a responsive and engaging driving experience. Paired with rear-wheel drive, this powertrain provides classic full-size sedan driving dynamics, offering smooth acceleration, confident highway passing power, and balanced handling. The combination of the refined 3.6L 6-cylinder engine and rear-wheel-drive architecture ensures that every journey is met with effortless performance and poise, making it an exceptional choice for drivers who appreciate a traditional, smooth-riding sedan.
Step inside the cabin to find a premium environment highlighted by luxurious black leather trimmed bucket seats, offering exceptional comfort for both short commutes and long-distance road trips. This vehicle also features a front license plate bracket and comes with the manufacturer's statement of origin. Combining elegant styling, a spacious interior, and a strong rear-wheel-drive pedigree, this Carfax 1-Owner 2022 Chrysler 300 Touring L RWD represents an outstanding opportunity to own a legendary American sedan that refuses to compromise on style or substance.
