Description
Turn heads wherever you go with this striking 2022 Toyota Tacoma 2WD TRD Sport Double Cab. Finished in a rugged and highly sought-after Army Green exterior, this midsize truck perfectly balances off-road-inspired styling with everyday urban drivability. Inside, you are greeted by a modern, durable Cement/Black interior designed to withstand both daily commutes and weekend adventures. Built with Toyota's legendary reputation for reliability and resale value, this TRD Sport Double Cab with a 5-foot bed offers the ideal blend of street-smart styling and versatile utility for truck buyers who want to stand out from the crowd.
Under the hood, this 2022 Toyota Tacoma is powered by a robust 3.5L V6 engine paired with a smooth-shifting automatic transmission, delivering the perfect combination of responsive acceleration and proven capability. As a 2WD TRD Sport, it features a sport-tuned suspension that optimizes on-road handling and cornering, making it an incredibly comfortable and engaging daily driver. The 5-foot bed provides ample cargo space for hauling gear, tools, or recreational equipment, ensuring you have the utility needed for any task. Whether navigating tight city streets or highway cruising, this V6-powered Tacoma offers a confident and composed driving experience.
This specific truck comes loaded with premium packages and accessories that elevate its convenience and aesthetic appeal. The sleek Black Out Package (TMS) adds a stealthy, customized look, which is perfectly complemented by the LED fog lamps featuring black bezels. Inside, the advanced Technology Package enhances your driving experience with modern safety and connectivity features, while the all-weather floor liners and door sill protectors (TMS) keep the cabin pristine. For added security and convenience, the remote tailgate lock (TMS) integrates seamlessly with your key fob. Combining bold styling, advanced technology, and legendary capability, this Army Green 2022 Toyota Tacoma TRD Sport is ready to tackle your next journey in style.
