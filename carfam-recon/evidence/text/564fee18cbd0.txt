Description
Turn heads wherever the road leads in this striking 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 2WD Double Cab 143.5" LTZ w/1LZ. Draped in a premium Siren Red Tintcoat exterior, this light-duty pickup truck perfectly balances rugged capability with sophisticated styling. Step inside the cabin to find a refined Cocoa/Dune interior that elevates your daily drive, offering an inviting and comfortable atmosphere for both the driver and passengers. Whether you are navigating city streets or heading out on a weekend getaway, this Silverado 1500 delivers a commanding road presence and a premium truck experience.
Under the hood lies the heart of a true performer: the robust 6.2L EcoTec3 V8 engine with Active Fuel Management, direct injection, and variable valve timing. This formidable 8-cylinder powerplant provides exceptional horsepower and torque, giving you the confidence and utility needed for towing, hauling, and highway merging. Paired with a responsive two-wheel-drive system, this powertrain maximizes on-road dynamics and efficiency, making it an incredibly capable and dependable partner for both demanding workdays and effortless cruising.
This highly equipped LTZ w/1LZ trim comes loaded with desirable upgrades that enhance safety, convenience, and style. The exterior profile is elevated by striking LPO 22" 5-spoke high gloss black wheels with machined accents and functional LPO 6" rectangular black tubular assist steps for easy cabin entry. Additionally, the LTZ Plus Package adds premium convenience features, while the Enhanced Driver Alert Package provides advanced safety technologies to keep you secure on every journey. This 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 is the complete package for truck buyers seeking the perfect blend of V8 power, distinctive style, and peace of mind.
