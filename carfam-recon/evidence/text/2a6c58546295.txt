Description
Experience the pinnacle of electric vehicle innovation with this Carfax 1-Owner 2020 Tesla Model X Long Range Plus AWD. Adorned in the stunning Pearl White Multi-Coat, this luxurious SUV combines elegance with cutting-edge technology. The interior is tastefully appointed in a sleek Black color scheme, offering a sophisticated atmosphere for both driver and passengers.
Equipped with all-wheel drive, the Model X ensures confident handling and power delivery across varying conditions. Active cruise control enhances your driving experience by maintaining optimal speed on highways. Safety features such as the blind spot monitor and forward collision warning provide peace of mind on every journey. The lane departure warning and lane keep assist further enhance your control and safety, making every ride more secure.
Comfort is paramount with heated seats, ensuring a cozy drive during colder months. The addition of a towing package brings versatility for all your travel and hauling needs. Rain-sensing wipers adjust automatically, providing exceptional visibility in all weather conditions. Enjoy seamless connectivity with Bluetooth, making it easy to stay connected on the go. Parking is made effortless with the parking assist feature, guiding you into tight spaces with ease. Experience the future of driving with this remarkable Tesla Model X.
