Description
Elevate your daily drive with the sophisticated 2019 Mercedes-Benz C 300 Sedan. Finished in a striking Polar White exterior, this luxury sedan perfectly balances timeless elegance with modern athletic proportions. Step inside to find a refined Black interior that provides a dramatic, premium contrast, offering an inviting cabin designed for discerning drivers who demand both style and status on every journey.
Under the hood, the 2019 Mercedes-Benz C 300 Sedan is powered by an advanced 2.0L 4 Cylinders engine that delivers a responsive and engaging driving experience. This powertrain seamlessly pairs efficiency with spirited performance, ensuring smooth acceleration whether you are navigating busy city streets or cruising on the open highway. The precise handling and refined ride dynamics of this engineered platform showcase the heritage and quality that defines the Mercedes-Benz driving experience.
This particular 2019 Mercedes-Benz C 300 comes equipped with desirable features that elevate its convenience and visual appeal, including a vibrant 10.25" center display for intuitive control and a sleek black fabric headliner that enhances the cabin's sporty ambiance. On the outside, the exterior is accented by stylish 18" multispoke wheels with black accents and a set of 2 LED logo projectors that welcome you upon entry. Complete with blind spot assist for added peace of mind, this sedan is the ultimate choice for those seeking a premium luxury vehicle that makes no compromises.
