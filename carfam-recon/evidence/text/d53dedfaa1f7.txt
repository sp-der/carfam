Description
Elegant, efficient, and exceptionally styled, this 2022 Toyota Highlander Hybrid XLE FWD is the ultimate modern family SUV. Dressed in the stunning, eye-catching Wind Chill Pearl special color, this vehicle commands attention on any road while offering the legendary reliability and sophistication the Highlander nameplate is famous for. Perfect for daily commutes, school runs, or weekend getaways, this midsize hybrid SUV blends premium aesthetics with eco-conscious practicality to elevate your everyday driving experience.
Beneath the hood, the 2022 Toyota Highlander Hybrid XLE is powered by an advanced and highly efficient 2.5L 4-cylinder hybrid engine mated to a front-wheel-drive system, delivering an exceptional balance of smooth power and outstanding fuel economy. Designed to support your active lifestyle, this vehicle offers superb utility and cargo capability. It comes equipped with dealer-installed cargo cross bars, allowing you to easily secure extra gear or outdoor equipment on the roof, making it as functional as it is efficient.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a refined Graphite interior that prioritizes passenger comfort and everyday usability. You and your passengers will enjoy your favorite media through the premium audio system with 6 speakers, which delivers crisp, clear sound throughout the cabin. The interior is also thoughtfully equipped with carpet floor mats and a cargo liner to keep your floors pristine, while a convenient cargo cover keeps your gear secure and out of sight. Combining eco-friendly engineering with premium features, this 2022 Toyota Highlander Hybrid XLE FWD is the perfect choice for smart buyers seeking a versatile, stylish, and highly capable SUV.
