Description
Discover the perfect blend of modern style and rugged sophistication with this eye-catching 2021 Toyota RAV4 XLE Premium. Wrapped in the highly sought-after Lunar Rock exterior paint, this compact SUV commands attention on any road while offering a refined, premium aesthetic. Step inside to find a sleek and sophisticated Black interior designed to elevate your daily commute or weekend getaways. The exterior visual appeal is further enhanced by the striking blackout emblem overlays (tms), giving this popular crossover a distinctively sporty and customized edge that stands out from the crowd.
Under the hood, this reliable crossover is powered by a dependable 2.5L 4-cylinder engine, delivering a responsive and fuel-efficient driving experience perfect for both city streets and highway cruising. The front-wheel-drive (FWD) configuration offers smooth, car-like handling and confident traction, ensuring a balanced and comfortable ride. For added peace of mind during inclement weather or nighttime drives, this vehicle comes equipped with high performance LED fog lights (tms) to maximize your visibility. Whether you are navigating daily traffic or heading out on a road trip, this powertrain offers the reliable performance and efficiency that Toyota is legendary for.
Inside and out, this XLE Premium trim is packed with thoughtful additions designed for convenience and comfort. The cabin is kept pristine thanks to the included carpet mat package (tms), while the frameless homelink mirror (tms) adds a touch of modern tech and convenience for easy garage access. For those with an active lifestyle, the roof rack cross bars (tms) provide excellent utility, allowing you to easily secure extra gear, bikes, or outdoor equipment. Ready to elevate your driving experience, this incredibly versatile 2021 Toyota RAV4 XLE Premium is the complete package of style, utility, and reliability—making it the ideal choice for your next adventure.
