Description
Discover the perfect blend of modern efficiency and urban style with this exceptional Carfax 1-Owner 2021 Chevrolet Spark 4dr HB CVT 1LT. Draped in a sophisticated Nightfall Gray exterior, this eye-catching hatchback is the ideal choice for commuters and city drivers seeking a reliable, agile, and well-maintained vehicle. With its single-owner history verified by Carfax, you can drive with absolute confidence, knowing this stylish compact ride has been cared for from day one.
Under the hood, the 2021 Chevrolet Spark is powered by an efficient 1.4L DOHC 4-cylinder MFI engine paired with a smooth Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT). This front-wheel-drive powertrain delivers responsive handling and outstanding maneuverability, making tight parking spots and busy city streets effortless to navigate. Engineered to meet emissions federal requirements, this hatchback offers an eco-conscious and cost-effective driving experience without sacrificing the nimble performance you need for your daily routines.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a comfortable and distinctive Jet Black/Dark Anderson Silver Metallic cloth seat trim. Equipped with the 1LT Preferred Equipment Group, preferred pkg, and safety pkg, this Spark is loaded with convenience features like keyless entry, telematics, and a helpful backup camera. Stay seamlessly connected and entertained on the go using integrated Bluetooth and the Chevrolet Infotainment 3 system, which features an intuitive 7-inch diagonal color touchscreen AM/FM stereo. This 2021 Chevrolet Spark is a smart, feature-packed hatchback that delivers big value in a compact package.
