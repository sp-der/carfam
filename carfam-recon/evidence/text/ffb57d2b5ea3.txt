Description
Experience the perfect blend of rugged capability and refined luxury with this stunning 2019 Lexus GX 460 4WD. Finished in a breathtaking Starfire Pearl exterior, this premium SUV commands attention whether you are navigating city streets or embarking on weekend getaways. Inside, the cabin welcomes you with an exquisite Ecru interior complemented by elegant Mahogany trim, delivering an atmosphere of sophisticated comfort and craftsmanship that defines the Lexus ownership experience.
Beneath its stylish exterior, the 2019 Lexus GX 460 is powered by a dependable 4.6L 8-cylinder engine that delivers smooth, effortless performance. Combined with an advanced Four Wheel Drive (4WD) drivetrain, this SUV provides exceptional traction and confidence across various road conditions. Ready for utility and adventure, this vehicle comes equipped with a tow hitch receiver with a ball mount to accommodate your towing needs, while the sleek exhaust tip adds a subtle touch of athletic style to the rear profile.
Designed with daily convenience and cargo versatility in mind, this luxury SUV features the preferred accessory package and a cargo-area tonneau cover to keep your belongings secure and out of sight. Passengers in the back will enjoy enhanced convenience on short commutes and long road trips alike, thanks to the inclusion of two Lexus universal tablet holders. Whether you are looking for a highly capable family vehicle or a prestigious daily driver, this 2019 Lexus GX 460 4WD offers an unbeatable combination of utility, premium features, and timeless style.
