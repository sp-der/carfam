Description
If you are searching for a heavy-duty truck that perfectly balances rugged off-road capability with premium comfort, look no further than this stunning 2022 GMC Sierra 2500HD 4WD Crew Cab 159" AT4. Finished in a clean Summit White exterior, this powerful three-quarter-ton pickup is engineered to tackle demanding jobs and challenging terrain alike. Built for those who refuse to compromise on strength or style, the AT4 trim commands respect on the road while delivering the premium, high-end experience that discerning truck buyers demand.
At the heart of this 2022 GMC Sierra 2500HD is the legendary Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine (B20-diesel compatible), delivering the massive torque and horsepower required to handle your heaviest payloads. Paired with a robust Four Wheel Drive (4WD) system, this truck is designed to maintain traction and composure in challenging driving conditions. For those looking to haul trailers, RVs, or heavy equipment, this model comes equipped with the Gooseneck / 5th Wheel Package, ensuring it is ready for heavy-duty towing right off the lot.
Step inside the spacious Crew Cab to find a refined Jet Black interior highlighted by distinctive Kalahari Accents, offering a bold yet sophisticated cabin aesthetic. This premium environment is further enhanced by the AT4 Preferred Package, while the Driver Alert Package I adds advanced safety features for peace of mind on every journey. Riding on eye-catching 20" machined aluminum wheels, this highly capable truck represents the ultimate combination of heavy-duty utility and modern luxury.
