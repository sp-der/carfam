Description
Step up your family adventure with this stunning 2018 Dodge Durango GT AWD. Finished in a striking White Knuckle Clearcoat exterior, this bold midsize SUV seamlessly blends muscle-car styling with everyday practicality. Step inside to find a sophisticated and spacious cabin anchored by premium Black leather-trimmed bucket seats that offer both comfort and durability for all your passengers. Whether you are navigating daily school runs or embarking on a cross-country road trip, this versatile SUV makes a powerful statement on the road.
Under the hood, this 2018 Dodge Durango GT AWD is powered by a dependable and efficient 3.6L 6-cylinder engine that delivers smooth performance without sacrificing fuel economy. The advanced All Wheel Drive system provides exceptional traction and control, giving you the confidence to tackle rain, snow, or dry pavement with ease. Engineered for utility as well as comfort, this Durango features the Trailer Tow Group IV, giving you the heavy-duty cooling and towing capability needed to confidently pull trailers, boats, or powersports toys for your weekend getaways.
This well-appointed SUV is packed with high-value packages designed to elevate your driving experience. The Safety/Security & Convenience Group offers advanced peace of mind and effortless daily operation, while the Premium Group surrounds you with high-end amenities. Overhead, a power sunroof floods the cabin with natural light and fresh air at the touch of a button. If you are searching for a capable, stylish, and feature-rich SUV that refuses to compromise on utility or performance, this 2018 Dodge Durango GT AWD is the perfect vehicle to park in your driveway.
