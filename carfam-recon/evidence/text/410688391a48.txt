Description
Experience the pinnacle of executive luxury and athletic styling with this stunning 2019 BMW 7 Series 740i Sedan. Coated in a deep, sophisticated Black Sapphire Metallic paint, this flagship sedan demands attention on every road. This particular model elevates its commanding road presence with the highly desirable M Sport Package, giving it aggressive aerodynamic lines and a distinctly sporty stance. Complemented by a factory badge deletion, this sedan offers a clean, understated, and stealthy aesthetic that appeals to discerning drivers who value both high-end luxury and sophisticated subtlety.
Under the hood, the 2019 BMW 7 Series is powered by a refined and potent 3.0L 6-cylinder engine. This power plant is renowned for delivering a seamless blend of robust acceleration and whisper-quiet operation, making it ideal for both spirited highway cruising and effortless daily commuting. Engineered for precision, the powertrain provides immediate throttle response while maintaining the executive-level ride quality and handling dynamics that the 740i Sedan is legendary for, offering a perfect harmony of engaging performance and passenger serenity.
Step inside the cabin of this luxury sedan to find a meticulously crafted environment designed for ultimate comfort and sophistication. The interior features premium Black Dakota leather upholstery that wraps passengers in comfort, perfectly paired with the elegant Fineline Black wood trim for a modern, cohesive, and darkened aesthetic. Every detail of this cabin highlights BMW’s commitment to premium craftsmanship and driver-focused ergonomics. If you are in the market for a prestigious executive vehicle that offers unparalleled style, refinement, and a commanding presence, this 2019 BMW 7 Series 740i Sedan is the ultimate choice.
