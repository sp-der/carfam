Description
Step into the future of driving with this stunning 2018 Tesla Model 3 Long Range Battery AWD. Finished in a sleek Gray exterior and paired with a sophisticated Black interior, this all-electric sedan beautifully redefines modern automotive style and efficiency. Combining a minimalist aesthetic with cutting-edge engineering, this highly sought-after Model 3 offers the perfect entry point into premium electric mobility, making every daily commute or weekend road trip an exciting, emissions-free experience.
Engineered for exceptional capability and confidence in all driving conditions, this 2018 Tesla Model 3 Long Range Battery AWD features an advanced All Wheel Drive drivetrain. Powered by its high-capacity electric battery, this dual-motor setup delivers instant torque, seamless acceleration, and precise handling, ensuring a planted and responsive feel on the road regardless of the weather. With its impressive long-range capability, this electric vehicle maximizes your travel potential while providing a smooth, whisper-quiet ride that highlights the superior utility of Tesla’s electric powertrain.
Inside the cabin, this Model 3 is packed with premium comfort and advanced safety technologies designed to elevate your driving experience. Passengers can enjoy the open feel of the moonroof, relax in the comfort of heated seats, and stream their favorite playlists via Bluetooth through the premium sound system. Driving confidence is enhanced by a suite of driver-assist features, including a blind spot monitor, forward collision warning, lane departure warning, lane keep assist, and parking assist. Complete with high-visibility LED headlights, this exceptional electric sedan represents the perfect blend of safety, luxury, and sustainable performance for its next owner.
