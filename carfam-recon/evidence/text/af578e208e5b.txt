Description
Elevate your daily drive with this exceptional, Carfax 1-Owner 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid Sport AWD. Stunningly styled in a sophisticated Platinum White Pearl exterior paint, this highly sought-after hybrid SUV turns heads wherever it goes. Inside, a refined Black interior provides a clean, modern aesthetic that perfectly complements its athletic outer styling. Offering peace of mind with its single-owner pedigree, this late-model vehicle represents the ideal blend of modern efficiency, reliability, and striking design.
Under the hood, this 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid Sport AWD is powered by an advanced 2.0L 4-cylinder hybrid engine, delivering an optimal balance of spirited performance and impressive fuel efficiency. Combined with a capable All Wheel Drive system, this SUV provides exceptional traction and control, making it perfect for handling inclement weather or navigating diverse road conditions with ease. Whether you are commuting through busy city streets or embarking on a weekend road trip, the hybrid powertrain and intelligent AWD drivetrain deliver a smooth, responsive, and confident driving experience.
Packed with premium convenience and safety features, this Sport AWD trim elevates every journey. Experience elevated comfort with cozy heated seats and let the light in with the built-in moonroof, while bright LED headlights illuminate the road ahead with crisp clarity. Tech-savvy drivers will appreciate seamless Bluetooth connectivity and the convenience of remote start on chilly mornings. Your peace of mind is prioritized through an advanced safety suite, including active cruise control, a blind spot monitor, forward collision warning, lane departure warning, and lane keep assist. Do not miss your chance to own this incredibly well-equipped, efficient, and pristine 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid.
