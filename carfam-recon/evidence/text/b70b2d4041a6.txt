Description
For off-road enthusiasts and collectors alike, this 2010 Toyota FJ Cruiser 4WD manual offers the ultimate blend of rugged heritage and modern dependability. Finished in a striking Black exterior, this iconic trail-ready SUV stands out with its distinctive retro-inspired styling and commanding road presence. If you are searching for a highly capable, adventure-seeking utility vehicle that delivers an engaging driving experience, this manual-transmission FJ Cruiser is the perfect match for your next journey.
Under the hood, this 2010 Toyota FJ Cruiser is powered by a robust 4.0L 6-cylinder engine, delivering the reliable horsepower and torque needed to conquer challenging terrain. Paired with a rare and driver-centric manual transmission and a sophisticated Four Wheel Drive (4WD) system, this powertrain configuration grants you maximum control over your driving dynamics. Whether you are navigating steep mountain passes, crawling over obstacles, or commuting in inclement weather, this SUV's legendary utility and trail-proven chassis ensure confidence in every environment.
Inside, the cabin features a durable and functional Dark Charcoal interior designed to easily handle the demands of an active lifestyle. This vehicle comes well-equipped with both the Upgrade Pkg 2 and the Convenience Pkg, adding valuable versatility and ease of use to your daily drive. Complete with a 5-piece carpeted floor and cargo mat set to keep your cabin tidy, and configured with 50-state emissions compliance, this 2010 Toyota FJ Cruiser is fully prepared for nationwide road trips. Do not miss your chance to own a highly capable and distinctively styled piece of automotive adventure history.
