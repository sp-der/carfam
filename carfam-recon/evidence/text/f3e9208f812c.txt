Description
Experience the perfect blend of luxury and sporty styling with this stunning 2017 Lexus RC 350 RWD. Draped in an eye-catching Ultra White exterior, this luxury sport coupe commands attention on any road. Open the door to reveal a breathtaking Rioja Red interior accented with Silver Performance Trim, creating a bold, driver-centric cabin environment that feels both sophisticated and premium.
Under the hood, the 2017 Lexus RC 350 is powered by a robust 3.5L 6-cylinder engine that delivers smooth, responsive acceleration. Paired with a dynamic rear-wheel-drive (RWD) configuration, this coupe offers an engaging and athletic driving experience. Whether you are navigating daily commutes or carving up winding roads, the combination of 6-cylinder power and RWD agility ensures every journey is both refined and exhilarating.
This vehicle is loaded with premium features designed to elevate your time behind the wheel, including a navigation system w/Mark Levinson premium audio for crystal-clear sound and effortless guidance. Enjoy the open air with the moonroof w/power tilt/slide, while LED fog lamps provide enhanced visibility in adverse conditions. Driving confidence is further elevated by advanced safety and convenience technologies, including a pre-collision system and front & rear intuitive parking assist to make tight maneuvers effortless. Do not miss your chance to own this exceptional 2017 Lexus RC 350 RWD.
