Description
Step into modern sophistication with the striking 2019 Chevrolet Blazer AWD 4dr Premier. Dressed in a pristine Summit White exterior, this premium midsize SUV commands attention on any road, especially with the bold, athletic touch of the LPO black bowtie emblems on the front and rear. Inside, you are greeted by an elegant and distinctive Jet Black and Maple Sugar interior that perfectly balances luxury and sportiness. It is the ideal blend of head-turning style and upscale comfort, making this vehicle a standout choice for drivers looking to elevate their daily drive.
Beneath its sculpted hood, this 2019 Chevrolet Blazer is powered by a robust 3.6L 6-cylinder engine, delivering confident acceleration and smooth power delivery. Paired with a sophisticated All Wheel Drive (AWD) system, this SUV provides exceptional traction and stability across varying road conditions, whether you are navigating wet weather or heading out on a weekend getaway. For added utility, this model also includes the LPO cargo package to help organize your gear and the license plate front mounting package to accommodate regional driving requirements.
Inside the cabin, advanced safety and interior preservation take center stage to ensure a premium ownership experience. This vehicle comes equipped with the Driver Confidence II package, offering a suite of driver-assist technologies designed to enhance peace of mind on every journey. Keeping the luxurious Jet Black and Maple Sugar cabin pristine is made effortless thanks to the durable LPO floor liner package. Combining eye-catching design, capable V6 performance, and smart driver-assist features, this 2019 Chevrolet Blazer Premier AWD is ready to deliver an exceptional driving experience.
