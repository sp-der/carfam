Description
Discover the perfect blend of distinctive styling and everyday practicality with this eye-catching 2016 Kia Soul 5dr Wgn Auto +. Finished in a crisp Clear White exterior, this iconic multi-purpose wagon stands out on any road with its bold silhouette and modern urban aesthetic. It is an ideal choice for savvy buyers seeking a unique vehicle that combines a compact, easy-to-park footprint with surprising cabin space and a confident road presence.
Under the hood, this 2016 Kia Soul is powered by a capable 2.0L 4-cylinder engine paired with a smooth automatic transmission. This efficient powertrain delivers a spirited and reliable driving experience, making it well-suited for both daily city commutes and highway cruising. The vehicle's clever wagon design maximizes interior utility, offering a user-friendly ride height and flexible cargo capability that accommodates your active lifestyle with ease.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a cohesive Black interior featuring premium black upgraded cloth seat trim and protective carpeted floor mats. The sharp contrast between the striking Clear White exterior and the dark, comfortable cabin creates a timeless and inviting atmosphere. Ready to elevate your daily drive, this 2016 Kia Soul offers an exceptional balance of personality, passenger comfort, and value.
