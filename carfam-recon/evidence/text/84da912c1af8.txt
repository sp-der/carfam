Description
Introduce the 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee RWD 4dr Limited, a premium midsize SUV that effortlessly blends sophisticated styling with daily versatility. This vehicle makes a strong first impression with its striking Maximum Steel Metallic Clearcoat exterior, a refined color that highlights the SUV's bold lines and athletic stance. Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a sleek, driver-oriented Black interior designed for lasting comfort. For buyers seeking a stylish and premium daily driver with undeniable road presence, this Grand Cherokee Limited delivers an exceptional package.
Under the hood, this SUV is equipped with a capable and reliable 3.6L 6-cylinder engine, offering a balanced mix of responsive power and highway efficiency. The rear-wheel-drive (RWD) configuration provides smooth, confident handling dynamics that make daily commuting and long road trips equally enjoyable. Additionally, this vehicle is configured with 50 state emissions compliance, ensuring it meets environmental standards across the country while delivering the dependable powertrain performance you expect from the Jeep brand.
This 2014 Jeep Grand Cherokee RWD 4dr Limited comes well-equipped with premium features that elevate your driving experience. The exterior profile is enhanced by stylish 20" x 8.0" aluminum wheels, while a single pane power sunroof lets natural light fill the cabin. Modern convenience is at your fingertips with the Uconnect 8.4AN system, featuring AM/FM/SXM/HD radio, Bluetooth connectivity, and integrated navigation to guide you on every journey. Combined with an immersive audio experience delivered by 9 amplified speakers with a subwoofer, this SUV represents an incredible opportunity for buyers wanting a feature-rich, comfortable, and highly capable vehicle.
