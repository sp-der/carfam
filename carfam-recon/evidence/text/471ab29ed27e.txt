Description
Looking for the ultimate dependable workhorse to elevate your business or daily operations? This 2019 Ford Transit Van T-250 offers the perfect blend of utility, maneuverability, and professional style. Finished in a clean Oxford White exterior, this 130-inch wheelbase, low-roof cargo van is designed to easily navigate tight city streets while still delivering the serious capability your business demands. Whether you are an independent contractor, fleet manager, or mobile technician, this Transit is built to handle your daily route with absolute confidence.
Under the hood, this versatile commercial vehicle is powered by a robust 3.7L 6-cylinder engine, offering a dependable balance of horsepower and torque to move heavy payloads. This powertrain is E85 Flex-Fuel capable, giving you added fueling flexibility and efficiency on the go. Boasting a substantial 9,000-pound GVWR, it is engineered to carry significant equipment and materials. Loading and unloading are made effortless with the practical swing-out right-hand side cargo door, streamlining your workflow even in cramped job sites.
Inside the cabin, you will find a functional workspace designed for all-day comfort, highlighted by Charcoal Black cloth dual bucket seats. Drivers will appreciate the convenience of cruise control with a message center to keep vital trip information easily accessible during long highway stretches. This model also features an appealing exterior upgrade package, along with fixed rear-door and fixed passenger-side cargo-door glass to significantly improve rearward visibility. Practical, reliable, and ready to work, this 2019 Ford Transit T-250 is the ideal asset to drive your business forward.
