Description
Looking for a dependable, stylish, and highly capable pickup that commands the road? This 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 2WD Crew Cab 143.5" LT w/1LT is the perfect blend of modern sophistication and rugged utility. Draped in a striking Summit White exterior, this truck turns heads whether you are pulling up to a job site or cruising down the highway. Inside, the refined Jet Black interior offers a spacious and premium cabin environment, making the Crew Cab configuration ideal for carrying family, coworkers, or extra gear with plenty of stretch-out legroom to spare.
Under the hood, this powerhouse is driven by a robust 5.3L 8-cylinder engine, delivering the muscle you need for demanding tasks while benefiting from the efficiency-boosting LT eAssist Package. Designed to maximize both power and utility, this 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 is equipped with a factory Trailering Package and a heavy-duty locking rear differential to ensure confident traction and impressive towing capacity when hauling your trailer, boat, or recreational toys. The rear-wheel-drive setup offers smooth handling and direct power delivery, making it a highly capable partner for both daily commuting and weekend getaways.
Rounding out its impressive utility is the highly sought-after All Star Edition, which elevates the truck's styling, capability, and convenience features. This vehicle also comes equipped with an LPO tri-fold soft tonneau cover, providing valuable protection for your cargo against the elements while enhancing the truck's overall aerodynamics. Combining V8 performance, smart hybrid-assist technology, and everyday practicality, this 2017 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 LT is the ultimate versatile truck ready to elevate your driving experience and tackle your next big project.
