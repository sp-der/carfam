Description
Experience the pinnacle of executive luxury and athletic styling with this stunning 2013 BMW 7 Series 740Li RWD. Dressed in a commanding Black Sapphire Metallic, this premium long-wheelbase sedan perfectly balances a prestigious road presence with the assertive aesthetics of the highly desirable M Sport pkg. Designed for those who demand both sophisticated elegance and a driver-focused attitude, this flagship vehicle stands out in any setting, from the corporate valet to the open highway.
Under the hood, the 2013 BMW 7 Series is powered by a refined and potent 3.0L 6 Cylinders engine that delivers the smooth, effortless power BMW is renowned for. Operating on a classic rear-wheel-drive platform, this sedan offers a dynamic and engaging driving experience that beautifully handles its generous size. The athletic nature of this luxury sedan is further emphasized by its staggered wheel setup, featuring 20" x 8.5" front & 20" x 10.0" rear double-spoke light alloy m wheels (style 303m) that provide excellent road grip and an aggressive, planted stance.
Step inside the cabin to find a sanctuary of absolute comfort and high-end craftsmanship. The interior features premium Black Dakota leather seat trim, offering a tactile experience and exceptional support for both long-distance highway cruising and daily commutes. Thanks to the extended wheelbase of the 740Li trim, passengers enjoy first-class legroom that defines the executive sedan class. Do not miss the opportunity to own this beautiful combination of executive luxury and M Sport performance—contact us today to experience this exceptional machine firsthand.
