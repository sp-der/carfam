Description
If you are searching for a midsize truck that perfectly balances rugged capability with modern style, this 2020 Ford Ranger XL 4WD SuperCrew is the ideal choice. Finished in a sophisticated Magnetic exterior paint, this pickup commanding attention whether parked on a job site or navigating city streets. With its spacious SuperCrew cab configuration and a practical 5-foot box, this truck offers the perfect blend of passenger space and cargo utility, making it a highly versatile option for daily commutes and weekend getaways alike.
Under the hood, this pickup is powered by the advanced 2.3L EcoBoost 4-cylinder engine, delivering a potent mix of efficiency and power. Paired with a robust Four Wheel Drive (4WD) drivetrain, the 2020 Ford Ranger is engineered to confidently tackle challenging weather conditions and off-road terrains. Whether you are utilizing the utility of the 5' box for hauling gear or navigating muddy trails, this truck's capable powertrain ensures you have the performance and traction required to get the job done.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a refined Ebony interior designed for both durability and comfort. This vehicle comes equipped with the Equipment Group 101A Mid package, adding key conveniences to elevate your driving experience. The cabin floor is well-protected and stylish, featuring carpet flooring with floor mats along with a practical tray style floor liner to handle dirt and debris. Completed with a front license plate bracket, this dependable 2020 Ford Ranger XL 4WD SuperCrew is ready to deliver a premium driving experience for its next owner.
