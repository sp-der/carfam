Description
Step up to exceptional style and utility with this striking 2016 Ram 1500 2WD Crew Cab 140.5" Big Horn. Finished in a pristine Bright White Clearcoat, this half-ton pickup commands attention on the road while offering the expansive passenger space of a full Crew Cab configuration. Equipped with the Quick Order Package 28S Big Horn, this truck perfectly balances rugged capability with daily drivability, making it an ideal choice for buyers seeking a versatile vehicle that looks as good as it performs.
Under the hood, this 2016 Ram 1500 is powered by the efficient 3.0L V6 Turbo Diesel engine, delivering impressive torque and capability for your daily drives and towing needs alike. Paired with a smooth-shifting 8-speed automatic (8HP70) transmission, the rear-wheel-drive powertrain provides a refined and responsive driving experience. Ready to work, this truck comes equipped with the Trailer Tow Mirrors & Brake Group, ensuring you have the control, safety, and visibility needed to haul your trailers and toys with complete confidence.
Inside the cabin, you are welcomed by a comfortable and spacious Diesel Gray/Black interior designed to keep both driver and passengers relaxed on long journeys. The tech-savvy dashboard features the premium Uconnect 8.4 NAV system, putting intuitive navigation, connectivity, and entertainment right at your fingertips. With its potent diesel efficiency, robust towing upgrades, and bold presence, this 2016 Ram 1500 Big Horn is the ultimate pre-owned pickup ready to tackle your next adventure or workday.
