Description
Get ready for your next adventure with this stunning 2016 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited 4WD 4dr Sport. Finished in a striking Bright White Clearcoat exterior paired with a classic Black interior, this iconic SUV perfectly blends rugged styling with everyday utility. Designed for those who demand both versatility and a commanding road presence, this four-door Sport model offers the spaciousness and legendary open-air freedom that makes the Jeep brand famous.
Under the hood, this capable trail-crawler is powered by a robust 3.6L 6-cylinder engine, delivering the dependable horsepower and torque needed to conquer any path. Power is routed through a smooth-shifting 5-speed automatic transmission (W5A580) to a highly capable four-wheel-drive system, giving you the traction and control to confidently handle adverse weather and rough terrain. Riding on durable 16" x 7.0" styled steel wheels, this 2016 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited is engineered to handle both the daily grind and off-road excursions with ease.
Equipped for comfort and convenience, this vehicle features a premium black 3-piece hard top that provides excellent cabin insulation while remaining easily removable for open-air driving. Inside, the Connectivity Group keeps you seamlessly in touch with your devices, while Sirius satellite radio provides a wealth of entertainment options for the road ahead. Whether you are navigating city streets or exploring remote trails, this beautifully configured 2016 Jeep Wrangler Unlimited Sport offers the perfect combination of legendary capability and modern features to elevate every journey.
