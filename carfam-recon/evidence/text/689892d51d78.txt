Description
If you are searching for a full-size SUV that perfectly blends commanding road presence with sophisticated style, this 2012 Chevrolet Tahoe 2WD 4dr 1500 LT is the ultimate choice. Draped in a striking Black exterior, this iconic vehicle demands attention on every road, making it ideal for families and adventure-seekers alike. With its sleek styling and highly sought-after LT trim, this Tahoe offers a premium driving experience for buyers who refuse to compromise on style, space, and versatility.
Under the hood, the 2012 Chevrolet Tahoe is powered by a dependable 5.3L 8-cylinder engine, delivering the robust V8 performance and smooth power distribution necessary for confident highway cruising and everyday driving. This capable rear-wheel-drive SUV is fully equipped for utility, featuring the Heavy-Duty Trailering Package to make hauling your favorite recreational toys or trailers simple and secure. To maintain its clean, streamlined aesthetic when the tow hitch is not in use, it includes the LPO trailer hitch closeout, seamlessly blending rugged capability with sophisticated design.
Inside, the cabin welcomes you with a refined Ebony interior designed for ultimate passenger comfort, elevated by the premium convenience of the Luxury Package. The exterior aesthetic is further enhanced by eye-catching 20" x 8.5" polished aluminum wheels that give this SUV an athletic, upscale stance on the road. Practical touches like the LPO rear cargo mat help protect your cargo area from daily wear and tear, ensuring this vehicle remains pristine for years to come. Do not miss the opportunity to get behind the wheel of this exceptionally well-equipped 2012 Chevrolet Tahoe LT—contact us today to schedule a test drive.
