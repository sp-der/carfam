Description
Experience the pinnacle of light-duty pickup luxury with this stunning 2019 Ford F-150 Limited 4WD SuperCrew 5.5' Box. Draped in a commanding Agate Black exterior, this premium truck makes an undeniable statement on the road while offering the ultimate in passenger space and comfort. Step inside the spacious cabin to find the exclusive, sophisticated Camelback interior, designed to elevate every journey with its distinct style and premium atmosphere.
Beneath the hood lies the heart of a champion: the high-performance 3.5L V6 EcoBoost High Output engine. Delivering exceptional power, this robust 6-cylinder powerplant is paired with a rugged Four Wheel Drive (4WD) system, ensuring confident traction and capability across diverse terrains and weather conditions. Ready to work or play, this F-150 is equipped with a comprehensive trailer tow package to simplify your hauling needs, while the 5.5-foot cargo box features a durable Tough Bed spray-in bedliner to protect your truck bed from rugged gear and heavy payloads.
Utility meets effortless convenience with the integrated tailgate step featuring tailgate lift assist, making accessing the cargo bed easier than ever. Additionally, this model is configured with a California emissions system, meeting strict environmental standards without sacrificing its legendary capability. Combining elite styling, relentless powertrain performance, and top-tier utility, this 2019 Ford F-150 Limited represents the absolute best of the modern American pickup. Do not miss your chance to put this exceptionally capable and luxurious truck in your driveway today.
